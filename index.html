About InAmigos Foundation

InAmigos Foundation is a Section 8 registered non-profit organization founded on September 23, 2020 by Mr. Govind Shukla.

The organization works across India to create meaningful social impact through education, community welfare, environmental sustainability, women empowerment, animal welfare and skill development.

Our Social Impact

Stories of change, resilience, and hope.

📚

Education

Breaking the cycle of poverty through learning opportunities. We believe that every child deserves access to quality education. Through our initiatives, we are lighting the spark of knowledge in young minds, giving them the tools to build a brighter future.

👩🏽

Women Empowerment

Helping women become financially independent and self-reliant. By providing skill development workshops, financial literacy programs, and awareness sessions, we are fostering a generation of empowered women ready to lead their communities.

🌱

Environment

Creating a greener future through sustainability efforts. Our environmental campaigns focus on mass tree plantation drives, plastic reduction awareness, and community-led conservation to protect our planet for generations to come.

🐕

Animal Welfare

Providing care, shelter, and compassion to animals. We run daily feeding drives for strays, facilitate medical treatments for injured animals, and promote a culture of kindness towards all living beings.

🤝

Community Service

Serving people in need through direct action. From distributing meals and warm clothing to the homeless, to organizing health camps, we are constantly working on the ground to address the immediate needs of underprivileged communities.

Our Journey

Tracing our milestones and continuous growth.

2020

Foundation Established

InAmigos Foundation was officially registered, taking the first step towards creating a meaningful social impact.

2021

Expansion of Programs

Expanded our community programs, reaching more individuals through local initiatives and volunteer drives.

2022

Growth of Key Initiatives

Significant scaling of Project Bachpanshala and Project Jeev, amplifying our impact in education and animal welfare.

2023

National Expansion

Successfully expanded our operational presence across multiple states, bringing our mission to a national level.

2024

Volunteer Surge

Witnessed an incredible increase in volunteer and internship participation, strengthening our grassroots presence.

2025-Present

Continued Growth

Continuing our mission with nationwide impact, striving daily to create a more inclusive and compassionate society.
